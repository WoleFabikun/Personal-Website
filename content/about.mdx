Hey I’m Lu, a software engineer and entrepreneur focused on building reliable, scalable systems/websites, and AI-powered products. I have a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and currently work as an engineer at a well-known large financial institution, where I design production systems that operate under real performance and reliability constraints.

I’ve had the opportunity to work on projects at the intersection of technology, automation, and social impact. One of my more notable contracts is [PaidLeave.ai](https://paidleave.ai/), an AI chatbot built in collaboration with Moms First and OpenAI to simplify complex paid family leave policies in all states that offer it. The project has been featured in [Forbes](https://forbes.com/sites/gemmaallen/2024/01/15/reshma-saujani-and-openai-bridge-gap-for-moms-with-new-paid-leave-tool/?sh=64170bc64122), [TIME Magazine](https://time.com/6342280/ai-paid-leave-social-good/), and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/12/05/openai-ai-chatbot-paid-leave-reshma-saujani/), and is actively expanding.

I was also involved with Novy, a generative AI collective formed to help companies gain real competitive advantages using modern language, audio, and image models. Our work ranged from automation pipelines to end-to-end AI products,  partnering directly with organizations, such as the NBA and Ticketmaster, exploring how AI can be applied responsibly and effectively.

Beyond AI, I build websites, internal tools, APIs, dashboards, and backend systems designed to reduce manual work and scale with growing usage. I care deeply about clean architecture, maintainability, and shipping software that continues to work long after the initial launch.

If you’re looking for someone who can bridge technical depth with real-world execution, you’re in the right place.
